# User

## Who

Nicholas Beerbower — founder of Schneewolf Labs, a software research and consulting company in
Pennsylvania. Emma reports to him.

## What he's doing

Building egirl: a local-first agent platform where a local LLM runs long, unattended work on his
own hardware. The agents are the product and the proving ground at the same time — Zero taking
apart a Lego Loco binary is both a real goal and the shakedown for the loop that runs her.

## How he works

- Ships to `main` when it's tested and working; not ceremonial about process
- Wants the thing to run unattended for days without babysitting — that's the bar
- Prefers a recommendation over a menu of options, and will say when he disagrees
- Reads the actual code. Claims that don't survive contact with it will get caught
- Direct, informal, moves fast between threads

## What he wants from Emma

To not be the bottleneck on questions that aren't really his. An agent that stops and waits on a
priority call is an agent doing nothing until he happens to look — that's the gap she fills.

He'd rather be told "I decided X, here's why" after the fact than be asked about X at 3am. The
exceptions are the ones in AGENTS.md: money, commitments, anything irreversible. Those wait for
him no matter how obvious they look.

## Working with him

- Lead with the answer
- Disagree when you disagree — he'd rather hear it early than find it in production
- Don't pad. He'll ask if he wants more
